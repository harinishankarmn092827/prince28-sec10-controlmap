# ControlMap – Project Questions

This document contains the six questions used to evaluate the ControlMap application's ability to answer cybersecurity framework and compliance-related queries.

## Question 1: NIST CSF 2.0 Functions

**Question:**

What are the six functions of NIST CSF 2.0?

**Expected answer:**

Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, and Govern.

## Question 2: NIST SP 800-53 AC-2

**Question:**

What does SP 800-53 control AC-2 require?

**Expected answer:**

Explain the Account Management control, including account creation, enabling, modification, disabling, removal, monitoring, and account management responsibilities.

## Question 3: SEBI CSCRF Entity Categories

**Question:**

Which entity categories does SEBI's CSCRF define?

**Expected answer:**

Identify the entity categories and explain their applicability based on the official SEBI CSCRF circular and applicable clarifications.

## Question 4: Access-Control Crosswalk

**Question:**

We are a mid-size stockbroker. For CSCRF's access-control requirements, which CSF 2.0 outcomes and 800-53 controls match, what evidence should we prepare, and what changes if we become a Qualified RE?

**Expected answer:**

- Identify relevant CSCRF access-control requirements.
- Map them to related NIST CSF 2.0 outcomes.
- Identify corresponding NIST SP 800-53 controls.
- Explain expected audit evidence.
- Compare applicability between Mid-size and Qualified RE categories.

All mappings must come from reviewed structured data.

## Question 5: Incident Response Comparison

**Question:**

Compare what CSCRF and NIST CSF 2.0 say about incident response and reporting.

**Expected answer:**

Compare incident response requirements, reporting expectations, responsibilities, timelines, and related controls using official framework references.

## Question 6: Unanswerable Question

**Question:**

Will our auditor pass us this year?

**Expected behavior:**

The application must not predict or guarantee an audit outcome.

Instead, it should explain that an audit result depends on actual implementation, evidence quality, compliance status, and auditor assessment.

It should offer to evaluate known gaps and available evidence.

## Question 7: Access Management Evidence

What documents and records should a mid-size stockbroker maintain to demonstrate compliance with access management requirements?

## Question 8: Framework Mapping Validation

How can ControlMap verify that a mapping between NIST CSF 2.0, NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5, and SEBI CSCRF is accurate?

## Question 9: Regulatory Applicability

How does SEBI CSCRF applicability change across different entity categories, and how can an organization verify which requirements apply to it?

## Question 10: Evidence Gap Analysis

Given an organization's access-control policies and evidence documents, identify missing evidence, weak evidence, and requirements that need further review.
## Evaluation Principle

The application must distinguish between:

- Verified regulatory requirements.
- Reviewed framework mappings.
- Evidence recommendations.
- AI-generated explanations.
- Questions that cannot be answered reliably.

The application must never invent regulatory requirements or guarantee compliance outcomes.
